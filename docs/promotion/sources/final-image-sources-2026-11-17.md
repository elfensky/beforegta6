## Facts per card

- **spacex-ipo.** A Falcon 9 lifts off from Pad 39A on 30 March 2017 (SES-10). SpaceX released the photo as CC0.
- **ram-shortage.** A Corsair Vengeance RGB DDR5 kit, 2x16 GB, 6000 MT/s. Both modules read "DDR5". Attribution and share-alike apply.
- **ai-overviews-glue-pizza.** An ordinary margherita pizza, photographed on 23 August 2020. The photo is not evidence of the AI answer. The alt text says "Illustrative photo".
- **flying-cars.** This is a frame from Tesla's official stream, uploaded by the Tesla channel on 2026-10-16 at 3840x2160. The Roadster is upside down over a Semi and two cars, and the on-screen altitude reads 25.2 ft. The HUD is cropped out, and the frame is scaled to 1920x1080. It is at about 11:05–11:10 in the stream; hwbusters gives ~10:56. Tesla's 46-second clip ["The Ramp"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7p5LxFdc-Qg) · [🔖 2026-11-17](https://linkding.lav.ren/bookmarks?details=6976) shows a second flip, off a vertical ramp.
- **gary-grand-theft-auto.** The episode is "Plankton's Old Chum", season 12 episode 24, first aired on 30 November 2019 ([TVmaze](https://www.tvmaze.com/episodes/1756722/spongebob-squarepants-12x24-planktons-old-chum) · [🔖 2026-11-17](https://linkding.lav.ren/bookmarks?details=6968)). The [wiki episode page](https://spongebob.fandom.com/wiki/Plankton%27s_Old_Chum) · [🔖 2026-11-17](https://linkding.lav.ren/bookmarks?details=6967) lists the music cue "Gary goes home in a car". The cue comes after SpongeBob asks about Chum Day, and SpongeBob sends Gary home. On screen, Gary slides out of frame toward the camera. The car never appears. The [wiki's 200-frame gallery](https://spongebob.fandom.com/wiki/Plankton%27s_Old_Chum/gallery) · [🔖 2026-11-17](https://linkding.lav.ren/bookmarks?details=6979) also has no frame with a car. The staged frame shows Gary leaving, with a Nickelodeon logo and a "BloxLogics" watermark. Reddit refused automated reads, so the three Reddit threads were not opened. Nickelodeon's own "Top 7 Moments" upload (`NsrLHG_oI3E`) is no longer on YouTube.
- **john-cena-heel-turn.** The screenshot comes from the public captioned embed. The post shows the GTA VI key art ("Coming 2025") with no caption and 532,539 likes on 2026-11-17. [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/john-cena-cryptically-posts-gta-6-image-without-explanation) · [🔖 2026-11-17](https://linkding.lav.ren/bookmarks?details=6971) gives the same post URL.
