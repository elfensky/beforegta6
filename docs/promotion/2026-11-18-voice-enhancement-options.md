# Voice enhancement options

Evidence date: 2026-11-18. Status: recommendations only. No service or purchase is selected.

Adobe Enhance Speech is the recommended first trial for the short original recording because its free allowance is sufficient.
Auphonic is the paid alternative when one-off credits and more processing controls are preferred.

| Service | Payment | Relevant features and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adobe Enhance Speech | Free plan available | Noise and echo reduction. Free audio uploads allow 30 minutes per file and one hour per day. Enhancement strength adjustment requires the paid plan. |
| Auphonic | €11 or US$12 before VAT for five hours of one-time credits | Noise and reverb reduction, voice equalization, and volume leveling. Credits do not expire. Auto-renew is optional and should remain disabled for a one-off purchase. The free tier adds an Auphonic jingle. |

The Auphonic price was verified in the official pricing page HTML: `plan-auphonic_onetime_5`, `data-price-euro="11"`, `data-price-dollar="12"`.
The minimum charge is three minutes of credits per production. The short take therefore fits within that minimum.
These are documented capabilities. Neither service has been tested on this recording.

**eli14:** enhancement is like better lighting for a performance. It can improve presentation while the performance remains the speaker's own.
